Privacy Policy

Semmozhi | Last updated: September 1, 2026

Semmozhi is a Tamil literature library application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Semmozhi does not require account registration and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location. The literature content is included with the app and can be read without creating an account.

Advertising

Semmozhi uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and use device information, identifiers, IP address, app interaction data, and approximate location derived from IP address to provide, measure, personalize, and prevent fraud in advertising. This information is handled under Google's policies.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob privacy information.

How Information Is Used

Information collected by advertising providers may be used to deliver ads, measure their performance, limit fraud, and comply with legal obligations. Semmozhi does not sell personal information or use it to create user accounts or profiles.

Data Sharing

Semmozhi does not share personal information directly with third parties. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its partners as described in their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Semmozhi is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can review the concern.

Your Choices

You can control ad personalization through your device's privacy and advertising settings. Depending on your region, Google may also provide consent and privacy controls before ads are shown. You may uninstall the app at any time.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app or applicable privacy requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated effective date.
